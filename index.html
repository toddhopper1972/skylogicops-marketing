Client Portal

Your Clients Get a Portal Too

Every job comes with a shareable client portal link — no login required. Your clients open a clean, branded page that shows job status, flight logs, and any attached proposal.

Proposals over $1,000 include built-in e-signature with typed or drawn signature capture. Clients accept or decline right from the portal, and you're notified instantly. No more emailing PDFs back and forth.

Shareable link — no client login required
Job status, flight logs, and notes visible in real time
Built-in e-signature with typed or drawn signature capture
One-click Save as PDF for client records
Branded with your company name, logo, and contact info
How It Works
1. Create a job and generate a portal link from the Client Portal Management page
2. Share the link with your client by email or message
3. Client views job status, flight logs, and any attached proposal
4. Client accepts or declines the proposal with e-signature
5. You're notified instantly — no follow-up calls needed
